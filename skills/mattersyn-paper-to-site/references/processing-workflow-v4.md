## Source sufficiency

Use the existing screened-pass local documents only. Read preparation sections, continuation paragraphs and the results needed to identify the product. Prefer existing source text, page renders and accepted evidence receipts. When a supplied main article refers to an available local SI for experimental details, inspect that SI before claiming the paper lacks a recipe. Verify its identity; do not assume a filename proves a match. Main/SI pairing is not a prerequisite to beginning assessment, and no new document acquisition is authorized.

A buildable contribution needs an identifiable material and inputs, an operational preparation with meaningful quantities and conditions, and an explicit connection to a reported structural outcome. Decide sufficiency for the actual method: an essential precursor charge, composition, thermal program or size-selecting condition can matter more than the number of filled fields. A missing optional unit cell, complete atomic coordinates, additional morphology image or ancillary measurement is not by itself a reason to skip a useful recipe. A substantial consolidation or annealing method may qualify with that specific scope; never describe it as an extraction of the upstream synthesis when that synthesis is absent.

Use three outcomes:

- **Eligible for regular integration:** sufficient preparation and product evidence exist; preserve all supported variants, then finish extraction, independent audit and shared material integration.
- **Skip: insufficient source information:** no usable preparation, essential preparation details are absent from the available source scope, or no recipe-to-structure assignment can be established. Save a concise private reason and exact source locators. Do not create a placeholder material page.
- **Technical or identity hold:** an unreadable file, parser limitation, missing local file, ambiguous document identity or unresolved evidence conflict prevents the decision. This does not establish that the paper lacks information and does not count as completed processing.

Do not omit known information to make a paper eligible or manufacture skip reasons to reach throughput. Conflicting reported measurements can be shown side by side when their source and specimen assignments are established. Keep unresolved assignments explicit.
