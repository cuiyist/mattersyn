# Preliminary publication first

**Superseded:** the owner retired public preliminary publication and changed the target to processed papers, including evidenced skips. Follow [processing-workflow-v4.md](processing-workflow-v4.md). The following text is retained as historical workflow context, not current publication permission.

Adopted at the owner's September 28 request to pause, revise both workflow and
first-publication quality, then resume toward 20 distinct published papers/hour.
This supersedes the older requirement to finish an independent full-source audit
and bespoke Reader assets before every first publication. It does not weaken
the requirements for a contribution labelled gold or calibrated silver.

## What changes

The default intake deliverable is a **preliminary synthesis contribution**: one
coherent, source-checked method with its ingredients, ordered procedure,
quantitative conditions, a source-linked structural outcome, precise locators
and explicit missingness. It is not a title card or an arbitrary group of scalar
facts. Read the selected method and the results needed to understand its product,
including definitions, continuation paragraphs and relevant captions. If the
recipe or product assignment cannot be established, hold that item and move on.

The first release may cover one declared route rather than every route in a
paper. State omitted variants, unread SI and unextracted sections. Additional
variants remain separate recipe/sample records when subsequently extracted;
they do not become additional published papers. Main and SI retain their
document identities, but one primary paper is credited only once.

Every preliminary page says **not independently audited**, **accuracy unmeasured**
and **excluded from training-ready data**. A source check by the extracting agent
is not an independent scientific audit. Preliminary linked outcomes stay out
of verified recipe–structure pair totals until independently accepted. Never
relabel earlier incomplete scalar notes as completed contributions.

Gold keeps its independent scientific review. Calibrated silver keeps the
confirmed 98% / 95% / 90% field thresholds and independently accepted held-out
calibration. Neither accuracy nor calibration is established by this workflow.

## Work allocation

Use three parallel source-scoped extractors plus one integration owner within
the four-worker limit and five active claims. An extractor returns a compact
public candidate and private source evidence together, directly in the shared
preliminary schema. Do not create separate per-paper website generators,
transport plans, presentation sidecars or release manifests for this lane.
Existing accepted gold packages are preserved for later additive integration.

After root accepts an immutable handoff, close its active extraction claim and
track it in the separate release queue. Extractors can prepare the next private
batch while the owner builds or deploys the accepted batch. Keep no more than
five active source claims and five unintegrated ready handoffs; all stages remain
visible in private state, and queued work has no publication credit.

Prioritize sources with readable methods, explicit products and unambiguous
recipe-to-product links. Complex multi-step or ambiguous sources go into an
enrichment/hold queue with a reason; do not erase their information to fit the
time budget. Main text or SI alone can be a declared scope. New scoped claims from the existing screened local collection resume after
engineering approval. Do not resume new external acquisition, downloads or
corpus-wide screening.

Plan for three extractors averaging six active minutes per straightforward
contribution, or 30 candidates/hour, leaving capacity for rework and deployment.
Nine minutes each is the upper service-time budget for 20 candidates/hour before
shared overhead. These are capacity targets, not measured speed or deadlines
that force unsupported decisions. Measure extraction, validation, integration,
deployment and rework separately. If the target is missed, use the measured
slowest stage to revise the next batch; do not lower the truthfulness standard.

## First-publication checks

1. Match the source identity and existing screened-pass receipt; pin the PDF.
2. Read and source-check the selected recipe and linked structural result.
3. Run the preliminary projector once: exact private page/quote/value anchors,
   complete field coverage, public schema, source locators and explicit status.
4. Merge valid candidates additively into the preliminary catalogue. A bad item
   does not hold the rest of a ready batch. Retain failed items privately.
5. Build and publish ready batches, aiming for five contributions or a 15-minute
   nonempty batch window. Run one successful full batch build, reuse its exact
   artifact and unchanged checks, and retain current CI, privacy and anonymous
   verification. A changed dependency or failed check needs a scoped correction.

The source check includes quantities, units, retained fractions, sample identity,
missing conditions and conflicts. A matching quote alone is not proof of the
interpretation. Do not fabricate measurements or silently fill blanks. Public
data uses concise authored summaries; source text, raw quotations, PDFs, page
renders, detailed audits and credentials stay local.

Preflight source identity and text readability before drafting a full candidate.
Prioritize existing pass documents without unresolved font-code strings. This
changes queue order, not scientific admission; difficult sources remain queued.
Correct an obvious author serialization error in a new version instead of
abandoning an otherwise useful source. Preserve failed receipts and count rework.

Where the printed page is readable but its text layer is wrong, the optional
visual-transcription receipt binds one exact scientific field to its original
PDF, raw page span, page image and explicit character edits. The extractor must
actually inspect the image. Keep original text unchanged; never apply global
digit or unit substitutions. Identity fields cannot use this mechanism. Its
mechanical validation does not establish visual accuracy or independent
scientific acceptance; preliminary status and training exclusion remain intact.

Use the shared projector's reviewed typography handling before treating a text
extraction problem as missing evidence. Exact, visually observed PDF punctuation
glyphs may be decoded through pinned private page/render receipts; degree-C
spacing is normalized only in the comparison view. Bounded numeric lists may
share a literal unit, while their specimen assignments still need the author's
source check. Unsupported symbols, units and legacy identifier syntax remain
explicit engineering holds. Preserve the known values and source observations;
do not omit them or change a DOI merely to pass validation. These mechanical
checks do not constitute independent scientific review.

## Deferred enrichment

The first page uses a single reusable academic template with precursors,
protocol, structural outcome, sources and limitations. Reuse existing exact
assets when available. Newly drawn apparatus, molecular conformers, reference
unit cells, morphology artwork, extra spectra, chemical intuition and complete
paper/SI coverage are enrichment tasks, not prerequisites for this preliminary
tier. Existing published source figures and full Readers are preserved.

Inspect a source figure when the extracted claim depends on it; do not infer an
unseen TEM shape or digitize an unchecked plot. Newly displayed source images
still need their exact attribution, sample assignment and release provenance.
Publisher permission remains factually unverified where applicable.

After publication, independently review preliminary records in a separate queue;
prioritize ambiguities, high-value variants and a reproducible sample across
sources. Correct affected records and withhold erroneous claims. A later gold
upgrade or visual improvement has zero new-paper credit. Training admission
remains a separate task.

## Measurement and continuation

Report distinct newly deployed and anonymously verified primary papers by tier:
preliminary, calibrated silver and independently audited gold. Separately report
fully reviewed papers and verified pairs. Count the union of primary sources,
not the sum of overlapping tiers. A successful build or local page is not a
publication. Record carried-over work and exact fixed measurement intervals.

The previous fixed hour ended with two new gold sources, not 20. Do not rewrite
that result. Start the next measurement only after this lane is implemented and
resumed; record engineering setup separately. The target remains unproven until
actual end-to-end publications demonstrate it.

Morning and two-hour reports remain disabled. Resume after the bounded workflow
revision and engineering checks, as the owner requested; then continue until a
new pause. Keep code, memory and reusable policy in GitHub at the next validated
source transaction. No paid APIs, cloud source processing, external document
transfer, external paper/model acquisition or GPU purchases are authorized.

## Reader discovery and count clarity

Homepage, source library, inventory and dataset must expose the union of reviewed and preliminary primary papers, with separately labelled tier counts and a direct link to preliminary recipes. Preserve reviewed material-family and verified-pair fields. Source upgrades count once; a preliminary source must not disappear from public discovery merely because its independent audit is pending. Build these totals from records and the validated catalogue, never by manually inserting a number.

Preliminary recipes also belong in the normal periodic-table discovery and material browsing. A separate collection link and a higher homepage counter are not sufficient integration. Attach a preliminary method to an existing material only through its explicit source-checked hub binding; otherwise expose a source-scoped material entry without guessing that two compositions or specimens are equivalent. Preserve review labels, missingness, recipe-to-product links and training exclusion in both paths. Reader discovery does not upgrade scientific review status or create additional paper or verified-pair credit.
