# Complete paper and SI review

For MatterSyn, completeness remains mandatory for recipe-bearing papers. A folder index or selected-recipe extraction does not establish full-paper curation. The September 19 instruction authorizes batches of up to five independently reviewed papers, each with its own ledger and independent audit, plus publication of several passed contributions together. Papers without synthesis recipes may leave the full-curation pipeline after evidence-based screening and an independent exclusion check; call this screening, not complete scientific extraction. The batch workflow and skip rules are described in [incoming-corpus.md](incoming-corpus.md). Report actual completed scope and remaining work without presenting a schedule as completed scientific work.

## Coverage ledger

Before marking supplied documents fully reviewed, verify main/SI identity and read every page, including captions, experimental sections, tables, unnumbered schemes, graphical abstracts and SI acquisition details. Visually inspect pages: extracted text can miss a minus sign, superscript, plotted legend or sample label. Record document role, source SHA-256, page count, and each page's text/visual review state. A missing or unmatched supplement remains a gap; it is not silently excluded from the claim.

Inventory all synthesis variants, precursor preparations, controls, aliquot series, workup branches, measurements, models and cited upstream methods. Every figure/table/scheme should have a disposition: extracted with locators and sample links; contextual/unassigned; or unresolved with a specific reason. Preserve original readable assets and their source/crop hashes. Text and table values can be transcribed; keeping a plot image is not raw-curve digitization. Do not imply that absent raw data, omitted conditions or unresolved references have been recovered.

## Variant and specimen audit

When deriving a variant from a common protocol, audit all representations: typed quantities, stock scopes, operation prose, material notes, target/product facts and displayed captions. A correct numeric field can coexist with stale copied prose. Mark inherited statements as inherited with their scope, and remove contradictory old amounts. Do not transfer a replacement reagent's concentration from the reagent it replaces.

Distinguish dry powder, aqueous dispersion, a recovered catalyst, a diluted optical sample and an early aliquot. Multiple optical dilutions are not synthesis replicates. Time windows, trajectory points, alternative formulations and scale-up claims should retain their meanings; do not tell readers to choose one when entries describe a time series. Record sample-state uncertainty instead of inventing a drying or transfer step.

Keep model-derived values, apparatus settings, reference-material constants and experimental outcomes separate. A proposed energy diagram is not measured electronic structure; an FFT is not an acquired SAED pattern. Preserve conflicting reported numbers or dimensional definitions with separate locators. Do not average them or silently repair a suspected typo.

For substantive extractions, use an independent audit of source claims and sample joins before publication. Record its actual scope: a targeted audit must not be described as a second full-page review. Fix findings in the authoring source as well as generated output. Source reading, independent audit, raw-data transcription and task-specific training eligibility remain separate statuses.

For scanned numerical supplements, track page/identity/continuity inspection separately from cell transcription and numerical audit. Preserve the complete original table and explicitly record any untranscribed cells or unresolved markers; a readable image does not establish a machine-readable numerical export. Do not clamp negative diffraction intensities or replace blank cells with zero. In crystallographic tables, retain the printed coordinate and displacement scaling, estimated standard deviations, space-group setting, and whether occupancy means atoms per unit cell or site fraction. Nominal composition, average scattering representation, partial/disordered occupancies and proposed charge-compensating species can differ; none by itself defines a unique ordered DFT structure.

For long scanned tables, add bounded page/block transcription chunks with raw decimal strings, cell locators and original-crop hashes. Preserve already audited chunks unchanged; a separate progress index can describe their combined scope. A distinct reviewer compares the printed cells, and any correction retains the previous transcription, a cell-level delta and a recheck against the corrected hashes. Count actual visually checked cells separately from automated consistency checks.

An original PDF can still render incorrectly in a particular engine, especially degree signs and scientific symbols. When that occurs, compare another renderer against the same verified PDF and save faithful replacement assets with their renderer and crop provenance. Preserve the previously audited extraction assets; do not paint corrected numerals or symbols onto an image and present it as the original.

When a figure, table or prose passage disagrees with another, attach the conflict to each affected exported field and sample association as well as the source-level note. Keep treatment-state labels distinct from final measurement conditions. Bind scientific audit results to the exact facts, inventories and asset hashes checked; after corrections, verify the changed scope before updating the audit. Passing source extraction does not complete canonical, illustration, browser or publication gates.

An intervening preparation step can make a treatment label insufficient to establish the measurement condition. For example, base addition after acid aging leaves the final pH unknown unless it was reported; do not assert either equality or a numerical difference. Link each figure to supported canonical specimen/model contexts, deduplicate repeated context pointers, and retain uncertainty about physical aliquot identity across techniques.

## Reference reuse and final presentation

Qualify reused molecular references against the exact reagent identity, stoichiometry and source-specific grade. A name lookup can return neutral ligand fragments with the wrong hydrogen count for a metal chelate; do not promote that result to a coordination structure. A converged illustrative conformer is not bond-specific validation. When reusing a shared model, preserve the audited base files and put source-dependent grade, isomer and hydration limits in scoped bindings. Inspect labels embedded in SVG artwork and model captions as well as registry metadata: a scoped binding cannot neutralize a different paper's grade or apparatus specification baked into the image. Verify that the actual viewer renders the correct scoped captions.

For reused atomistic assets, check chemically significant distances against the qualified reference as well as graph connectivity; a valid graph can have incorrect geometry. An external host unit cell must retain its database provenance without importing another paper's specimen labels, processing history or dopant coordinates. Keep free-molecule conformers, disconnected formal ions, symbolic polymers and measured specimen structures visibly distinct.

Each independently selectable control scene must expose that control's own reported concentrations and unresolved concentration basis. Information shown in a neighboring representative recipe does not complete the control. Preserve the author revision when an independent reviewer requests changed quantities, captions or geometry, and bind the final audit to the corrected render and input hashes.

Keep audit inputs immutable. If final presentation changes only a workflow-status sentence or a binding caption, preserve the prior audit and independently recheck the affected scope with current hashes. Do not imply that a targeted addendum repeats a full source review. Derive released counts from current canonical records and mark historical provenance as historical. After verified publication, refresh the private `incoming-paper-monitor/latest-publication.json` summary so the queue dashboard agrees with the released atlas.

Reader cards need readable academic prose, not compressed extraction notes. Derive quantitative displays from canonical fields, including operation environment and endpoint values; keep source-inventory payloads accessible through exact pointers and explanatory prose without presenting metadata objects as measured outcomes. A documented ratio display such as 500:1 may format a canonical relative scalar while preserving its unspecified basis. Audit those declared display transformations rather than requiring misleading literal scalar/unit text.

## Current implementation

Private review packages live under `research-assets/deep-review-20260917/<paper>/` with `coverage.json`, canonical records, original crops and audit notes. Public ledgers are sanitized into `recipe-atlas/data/paper-reviews/`; the public page is `paper-review.html?id=<canonical-source-id>`. Source-library and material-hub links expose complete review separately from selected recipes. `scripts/build_paper_reviews.py` checks page inventories and asset hashes. Canonical recipes, not coverage pages or original figures, feed training exports.
