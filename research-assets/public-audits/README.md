# Audit evidence inventory

This index preserves artifact identities, hashes and narrowly selected reported status/count fields from the pre-repair research archive. It is not a count of completed papers. Full source prose, working crops and unredacted audit payloads remain in the private evidence archive. Reviewed source scope and scientific findings remain in the published contribution records and Reader/Data views. Publication-only transformations have separate receipts; they do not recertify scientific audits.
