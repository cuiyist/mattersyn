PAPER EXPLORER

One paper. One clearly scoped recipe.

This page follows “Zinc Blende CdSe Core QDs,” on PDF page 2 (printed page 4720). The neighboring wurtzite and shell-growth recipes are separate experiments. The authors infer the ≈3 nm diameter from the absorption peak; a PL quantum yield is not given for this core procedure.

MOLECULAR DATA

Computed structures, visible provenance.

Molecular structures are labeled by their source and represent possible conformations, not a measured arrangement in solution. The free-acid structures shown here differ from the deprotonated ligands bound to a QD surface. The flask is an illustration; its configuration and liquid color are not reported measurements.
