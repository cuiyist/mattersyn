THE GROWTH STORY

Fast growth favors elongation.

The authors describe rapid growth along the wurtzite c-axis at high monomer concentration. As monomer is depleted, the short axis grows and the aspect ratio can fall. Further injections can sustain or recover rods.

WHAT THE PAPER MEASURES

Shape changes the optical response.

Figure 4 compares dot and rod spectra and shows polarized rod emission after alignment at 4.7 K. The approximately 1% room-temperature luminescence quantum yield is a general as-grown-rod observation, not assigned to a selected model here.

STRUCTURE PROVENANCE

Real connectivity. Clearly labeled models.

HPA and TBP use computed PubChem 3D conformers. TOPO uses a PubChem 2D depiction. Dimethylcadmium uses a connectivity schematic. Selenium is a solid reference, not a model of the dissolved stock species. CdSe uses an ideal wurtzite lattice from COD. Apparatus and social-preview artwork are illustrative.

UNREPORTED DETAILS

What cannot be filled in from this paper.

No numerical growth duration, stock preparation procedure, atmosphere, heating ramp, complete injection schedule, or purification protocol is supplied. The optimized variant does not repeat bath mass or post-injection temperature. Its 8% HPA value has an implied weight basis from the surrounding discussion, rather than an explicit repeated unit.

A historical research method, not a ready-to-run laboratory SOP. Dimethylcadmium handling requires specialist laboratory controls.
